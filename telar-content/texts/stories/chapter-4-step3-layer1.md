In this sense, this is a profoundly violent map. It is a map of land dispossession and of the transformation of the Indigenous agricultural landscapes into grazing pastures for Spanish estate owners—estates on which Indigenous people were forced to work. A few details on the map reveal the marks left by this process, such as the place that indicates the location of the former cacicazgo, or the small area allotted to the community’s *resguardo*, in contrast with the large estates owned by Spaniards.
